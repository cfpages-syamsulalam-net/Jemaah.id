# Article Catalog — Jemaah.id

Scope: 120 evidence-led Indonesian article briefs that help prospective Hajj/Umrah pilgrims and their families make safer decisions, prepare, respond to problems, and evaluate delivery. The catalog plans knowledge assets; it does not assert that prototype package, agency, price, rating, permit, or review data is real.

Domain boundaries: dynamic inventory and entity facts belong to `/search`, `/compare`, `/package/:id`, `/agency/:id`, `/hotel/:id`, and `/airline/:id`; transactions and private workflows stay in account/booking/partner/admin routes. Religious rulings require primary sources and named scholar review. Medical, legal, financial, visa, safety, and regulatory material is educational, time-stamped, and reviewed by the relevant professional or authority.

Catalog statistics: 20 parent topics; 120 planned articles; exactly six briefs per topic; 12 Wave 1 assets. No brief is a city/province swap. Existing-route collision audit is required again when `/panduan/<slug>` is implemented.

Priority legend: **P0** establishes trust/safety foundations; **P1** supports core decisions; **P2** deepens preparation and operational coverage; **P3** is useful long-tail/reference material. Waves are bounded editorial batches, not permission to mass-publish.

## First-wave rationale and measurement

Wave 1 contains JEM-01-01, JEM-01-03, JEM-02-01, JEM-03-01, JEM-04-01, JEM-04-02, JEM-04-04, JEM-05-01, JEM-05-05, JEM-06-01, JEM-11-01, and JEM-12-02. Measure valid indexation and canonical choice; impressions and clicks by distinct intent group; checklist starts/completions; transitions to `/verification`, `/compare`, and relevant product routes; qualified questions or leads; source/reviewer freshness; correction requests; and Search Console query overlap. Do not advance the next wave merely because pages are published or ranking: first confirm that the pages are accurate, used, non-cannibalizing, and leading readers to safer decisions.
